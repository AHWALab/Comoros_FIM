Likelihood that the flood depth exceeds a threshold, computed by matching every ensemble member to a library of 200 pre-simulated pluvial flood scenarios of its commune (RainyDay storms over the island hydrodynamic models, 30 m). Every commune of the country has its own library; the map shows the communes of the selected island side by side.

Very low, under 20 percent Low, 20 to 40 percent Medium, 40 to 60 percent High, over 60 percent Reported impact Commune tint: share of the commune with likelihood (light under 0.5 percent, dark over 4 percent)

Likelihood at a pixel is the share of the 50 StormLab members whose matched scenario reaches the depth threshold there. A commune is triggered when any of the 60 runs (10 analysis, 50 forecast) exceeds 1.0 m3/s/km2 of unit streamflow inside its window; quiet communes carry no likelihood. Overbank only removes pixels that are already wet in the reference (driest) scenario of each library, so what remains reads as hazard beyond the permanent water.
